https://github.com/MrTangLuyao/Bilibili-thread-ripper

The application adapts the bounded parallel Range, candidate scheduling and
response validation approach in C#, including its upos mirror host list and
relay (MCDN/PCDN) exclusion rules. Browser interception/UI code is not bundled.
